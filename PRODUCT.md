# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Affluent homeowners planning a new home or a significant renovation. They arrive
researching a design partner — often referred or arriving from a showroom, social,
or search — and are evaluating whether Novesso can be trusted with a high-stakes,
high-cost, long-lived space. Their context is considered, not impulsive: they are
comparing a small set of options and want evidence of taste, craft, and competence
before committing to a conversation. A secondary trade track (architects/designers)
exists via the audience-split section, but the primary voice speaks to end clients.

## Product Purpose

Novesso is a contemporary interior design practice with an Italian design language —
architectural layout and refined material systems (wardrobes, sliding systems,
partitions). Tagline: *Creare Vita Moderna*. The site exists to convert qualified
homeowners into **booked consultations**. Showroom visits and catalog browsing are
supporting intent-builders; the consultation is the conversion event. Success looks
like a visitor who feels they've found a discerning, capable partner — and books.

## Brand Personality

Confident, modern, understated. Quietly self-assured contemporary luxury where
restraint is the flex — the brand never shouts. Voice is precise and editorial,
warm rather than clinical, speaking peer-to-peer with a design-literate client.
Emotional goal: earned trust and aspiration without pressure. Italian heritage and
material honesty are present but implied through craft, not announced.

## Anti-references

- **Mass-market / IKEA-flatpack.** No budget, high-volume, assembly-furniture energy.
- **Generic SaaS landing.** No gradient hero, no repeated feature-card grid, no
  startup-template scaffolding (eyebrows on every section, numbered markers by reflex).
- **Loud luxury / gold-everything.** Gold is an accent, never a wash. No gaudy,
  ostentatious "bling." Prestige comes from space, typography, and material, not shine.
- **Cold corporate / sterile.** Avoid impersonal, clinical, faceless-enterprise tone.
  The practice has a point of view and a human hand.

## Design Principles

- **Restraint is the flex.** Confidence is shown through space, hierarchy, and
  what's left out — never through volume or ornament. When in doubt, remove.
- **Material honesty.** Imagery, texture, and real project work carry the luxury;
  the UI frames them and gets out of the way. Show the work, don't describe it.
- **Architectural rhythm.** Layout behaves like considered space — deliberate
  proportion, generous negative space, varied spacing for cadence, not uniform grids.
- **Earned trust, no pressure.** Every section builds credibility toward the
  consultation. Guide, don't push; the CTA is an invitation, not a hard sell.
- **Gold as punctuation.** The navy/cream foundation does the work; gold marks the
  one thing that matters in a view, never decorates broadly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**. Body text ≥4.5:1 against its background, large text ≥3:1 —
verify the navy/cream and any muted-text pairings rather than assuming. Full keyboard
navigation with visible focus states (consultation modal, mega-menu, mobile menu).
Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for all GSAP/scroll interactions with a crossfade or
instant fallback. Semantic markup and meaningful alt text on project/material imagery.
